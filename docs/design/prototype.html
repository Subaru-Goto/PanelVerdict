The analyst’s reading

The most common reason given: a percentage is a number a reader can act on, where “half price” makes them stop and translate. The readers who went the other way all pointed to “members” — it made the line feel addressed to them. If belonging matters to your audience, that is the thread to pull in a next pair.

This is a panel of synthetic readers, not a live audience. It says which line reads better to readers like these; it does not predict how many people will click.
